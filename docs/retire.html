When can I retire?

You save $2,500 every two weeks into covered-call income funds and reinvest every distribution (after 15% US withholding). This page projects the date your net monthly payout covers your target — using the funds' live trailing yields, refreshed daily by the same pipeline as the other pages.
Crunching the numbers…

Method. Monthly simulation: contributions arrive at (biweekly × 26 ÷ 12) per month; the portfolio pays its blended trailing-12-month distribution yield monthly; 15% is withheld and the rest is reinvested; fund prices drift by the chosen scenario. "Retired" = the month the after-tax payout first covers the target. Yields come live from data.json and data-gpiq.json; everything else runs in your browser, so drag the sliders freely.

What this is not. Not advice, and not a guarantee — distribution yields float with option premiums, and covered-call funds trade upside for income. Read the honesty box before planning your resignation letter.
